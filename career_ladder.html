Career Lattice — Flow v0 · draft

One shared rubric across every track — five buckets, weighted differently by role (click a bucket below for what it means). Analytics starts at Senior Analyst — there\u2019s no junior BI role — and promotes across into Analytics Engineering. Analytics Engineering and Data Engineering both start at L1 and climb Senior → Lead → Staff → Principal. Above Lead, Data Engineering forks three ways: stay generalist (Staff/Principal), specialize into Data Architect, or move into Management. Solid arrows are promotions; dashed arrows are on-ramp crossovers.

Lead is a three-way fork. From Lead Data Engineer, the path splits into generalist IC (Staff/Principal), architecture specialist (Data Architect), or people management — same altitude, different work.

Two kinds of track change. Senior Analyst → Analytics Engineer is a promotion — there\u2019s no junior BI role to cross from. Analytics Engineer → Data Engineer is an on-ramp crossover that resets to entry level, gated by the dbt Developer cert + ~6 months dbt client experience.

read 4

Certification minimums are written into the description, not a separate badge — the first Technical bullet at that level states the requirement plainly (Omni for Senior Analyst, dbt Developer for Analytics Engineer, one of Snowflake/AWS/GCP for Data Engineer).
